Introduction

The RTX 4080 Super sits at the top of NVIDIA's RTX 40 lineup, just below the flagship 4090. At $999, it aims to be the best GPU for 4K gaming without paying the 4090 tax. But does it justify $999? We compare it against the RTX 4070 Ti Super ($799) and AMD's RX 7900 XTX ($899).

Ray Tracing: NVIDIA's Clear Lead

In Ray Tracing titles (especially Cyberpunk 2077 with Path Tracing), the RTX 4080 Super nearly doubles the RX 7900 XTX's frame rate at 1440p Ultra RT — 72 FPS vs. 38 FPS. This comes from Ada Lovelace's more efficient RT cores and DLSS 3 Frame Generation support.

Is the RTX 4080 Super Worth $999?

The RTX 4080 Super delivers +18–23% more 4K FPS vs. the RTX 4070 Ti Super for a $200 premium. That's roughly 10–12% performance per $100 extra — quite efficient for the premium segment. The RX 7900 XTX at $899 is a strong middle ground: similar rasterization performance at lower cost, but falls behind in RT and DLSS quality.

Conclusion

The RTX 4080 Super is the best GPU for 4K gaming with Ray Tracing in 2026 at its price point. If you're gaming at 1440p, save $200 and get the RTX 4070 Ti Super — the FPS difference is barely noticeable, and you can spend those extra funds on a better monitor or peripherals. For 4K without heavy RT use, the RX 7900 XTX at $899 is a compelling alternative.
